Frequently asked questions

Construction

The click creates a point instead of opening the ring

The click missed the object. Ctrl Z, then aim at the stroke. Near a crossing, the click creates the intersection.

The tool stays active

Palette tools are persistent: Done, Esc or ✕ leave them.

The click builds nothing

A mode is active: eraser ( blue), step by step, magnets, or properties panel without a tool. Esc leaves it.

Clicking an empty spot creates no point

The palette is open: without a tool, the figure can only be manipulated. Take the Point tool, the first in the palette, or close the palette.

(2,5, 1) gives a point in space

The decimal separator is the point: (2.5, 1).

L = [1, 2, 3] is refused

An uppercase name makes a point, here in space. Write list = [1, 2, 3].

pi is an unknown object

Write PI or π.

An object cannot be named x or y

At the start of a line, they introduce an equation (y = 2x + 1).

Transforming a polygon

Choose the transformation, designate what defines it, then the polygon: its image is a polygon. To transform several, designate the center or the axis with Shift (Transforming an object).

Changing the radius of a circle created with one click

Drag the circle, or edit its line (Circle(O, r) with a number, a slider or a formula).

Deleting a point erased other objects

The dependents are deleted with it. Undo, then hide the point instead of deleting it.

3D and view

“3D view” does not return to the plane

An object leaves the plane z = 0; the prompt names it.

Rotating with the mouse

Right button; the left button moves the view.

Zooming on a tablet

Spread two fingers to zoom in, bring them together to zoom out. Drag both fingers together to move the view.

The traces have disappeared

They are cleared each time the view is moved or zoomed.

Files

A figure opened with ?url= does not load

The file must be shared with anyone who has the link, without a password, and ?url= must come last in the address (see Sharing a figure by link). OneDrive links from a school or work account are not supported, nor are sites that refuse automated downloads.

Incomplete import

The listed elements are not supported (DGPad and GeoGebra import); the rest of the figure works and can be saved.
